Preface

Welcome to MT-101: Mathematics Foundations I. This interactive digital textbook was designed as a support tool for students in the health sciences, especially those enrolled in Radiological and Diagnostic Imaging programs.

The main purpose of this material is to help students develop a strong foundation in fundamental mathematical concepts, promoting logical reasoning, problem-solving, and the application of mathematics in real-world health science contexts.

Throughout this book, topics are presented progressively through conceptual explanations, worked examples, practice exercises, and assessment preparation activities. In addition, contextualized applications in radiology, ultrasound, and medical imaging are included to strengthen the connection between mathematics and the student’s future professional practice.

This book was also designed with a hybrid and bilingual learning environment in mind. For this reason, the content is presented in both English and Spanish, facilitating access to mathematical and scientific terminology frequently used in university courses and clinical settings.

🧠 Book Philosophy

Mathematics should not be viewed only as a set of rules and procedures, but as a tool to understand, analyze, and solve real situations within the health sciences field.

Students are encouraged to use this book actively: read each section carefully, practice regularly, attempt problems before reviewing examples, and use the assessment preparation activities to reinforce learning before evaluations.

We hope this resource contributes significantly to your academic and professional development.

Introduction

Mathematics is an essential part of many disciplines within the health sciences. From medical image analysis to the calculation of doses, intensities, distances, and anatomical measurements, professionals in the field constantly use mathematical concepts to interpret information and make accurate decisions.

The course MT-101: Mathematics Foundations I. introduces students to the fundamental algebraic and numerical concepts that will serve as a foundation for future courses and professional applications. Throughout this book, students will develop skills related to real numbers, inequalities, exponents, algebraic expressions, polynomials, and linear equations.

🩺 Health Sciences Application

In radiology and medical imaging, mathematics is used to calculate exposure times, radiation intensities, image resolution, and anatomical measurements obtained through diagnostic equipment.

Each chapter has been organized to first present fundamental concepts, followed by step-by-step worked examples, practice exercises, and assessment preparation sections. This approach aims to strengthen both conceptual understanding and procedural skills.

The content also incorporates mathematical terminology in both English and Spanish to support students in bilingual learning environments and facilitate understanding of vocabulary frequently used in university and clinical contexts.

💡 Study Recommendation

Consistent practice is one of the most important tools for learning mathematics. Students are encouraged to complete the practice exercises before moving to the next section of the book.

Beyond memorizing procedures, this course seeks to develop mathematical reasoning, analytical skills, and confidence in solving academic and professional problems.

Structure of the Book

Each chapter follows a consistent academic structure so that students can move from basic concepts to applications and assessment preparation.

1. Learning Objectives

Brief statements describing what students should be able to do after completing the chapter.

2. Theory and Definitions

Clear explanations of the mathematical concepts introduced in class.

3. Worked Examples

Step-by-step examples showing how to solve problems using mathematical reasoning.

4. Practice Exercises

Exercises for students to complete independently after reviewing the examples.

5. Interactive Resources

Graphs, calculators, visualizations, and other tools that may be embedded in the HTML version.

6. Assessment Preparation

Practice aligned with quizzes, partial exams, and the final exam.

7. Practical Applications

Each chapter will have practical applications in different contexts and fields, including radiological sciences.

8. Learning Resources

Videos, mental maps, and infographics generated with NotebookLM based on the concepts explained in the chapter.
